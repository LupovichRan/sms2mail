# Store listing (English)

**App name:** SMS2Mail

**Short description** (max 80 characters, this one is 72):

> Forwards every SMS you receive to your email, via your own SMTP account.

**Full description:**

> SMS2Mail forwards the text messages you receive on this phone to an email address you choose, so you can read them on your laptop or any other device.
>
> How it works
> • Enter your SMTP server (Gmail works with an app password), your login and the destination address.
> • Switch forwarding on. Every incoming SMS (sender, text and time received) is emailed to you.
> • If the phone is offline, messages wait and are sent automatically when the connection returns.
>
> Privacy first
> • Messages go straight from your phone to the email provider you choose. The developer never receives them.
> • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers.
> • Encrypted connection only (STARTTLS, TLS 1.2 or newer). Your SMTP password is stored encrypted with the Android Keystore.
> • Excluded from cloud backups. Messages are not kept after sending.
> • Free and open source (MIT): https://github.com/LupovichRan/sms2mail
>
> SMS2Mail is not an SMS app and does not replace your messaging app. It only forwards a copy of incoming messages by email while you have forwarding switched on.
